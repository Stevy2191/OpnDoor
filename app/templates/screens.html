Setting up the tablet
  1. Open the screen's link in the tablet's browser.
  2. iPad: tap Share → Add to Home Screen, then open it from there so it fills the screen.
  3. Turn on Guided Access (Settings → Accessibility) to keep it on this page, and set Auto-Lock to Never.
  4. The page updates itself every minute. Nobody needs to sign in.
