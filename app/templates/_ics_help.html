Where do I find a calendar's iCal link?
  • Google Calendar: Settings → pick the calendar → Integrate calendar → copy Secret address in iCal format.
  • Outlook / Microsoft 365: Settings → Calendar → Shared calendars → Publish a calendar → choose "Can view all details" → copy the ICS link.
  • Booking systems (Skedda, Planning Center, Calendly, etc.): look for "calendar feed", "iCal", "subscribe" or "export" in its settings.

Treat the link like a password: anyone who has it can read the calendar.
